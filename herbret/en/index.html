The cabin

A private cove by Lake Stor-Degersjön

The cabin sits in a private cove with no neighbours, in the middle of the northern forests between Örnsköldsvik and Sollefteå. It sleeps up to 4 guests, with a sofa bed downstairs and a loft with mattresses. The kitchen has a gas stove and fridge, and the cabin is heated with gas. Down by the water there is a wood-fired sauna with a view and a boat for fishing.

In the middle of the northern forests, in a private cove with no neighbours.

Off-grid: the simple life

  • No running water. Bring your own drinking water.
  • No mains electricity. Charge your power bank beforehand.
  • Kitchen, fridge and heating run on gas.
  • A newly built shed and outhouse near the cabin.

By the water

  • Wood-fired sauna with a view over the lake, and relatively quick to heat up.
  • Boat for fishing, with many islands to explore on the lake.

Good to know

  • Free parking on the property.
  • Self check-in with a key lock box.
  • Check the coverage map in advance for mobile reception (4G/3G).
  • See the packing list further down for what to bring. The house rules are in the Airbnb listing.

Boat and fishing

Out on Stor-Degersjön

Take the boat from Herbret out onto Stor-Degersjön, a hilly lake with many islands. There are plenty of places to fish from, and the islands can be explored by boat.

Packing list

What to bring

Sleeping

  • Bottom sheets, one per person
  • Duvet covers or a sleeping bag
  • An extra blanket for chilly evenings

Hygiene and bathing

  • Towels (also for the sauna) and soap as needed
  • Toiletries and toilet paper
  • Insect repellent and sun protection as needed
  • Matches or a lighter for the wood-fired sauna

Food and drink

  • Drinking water for the whole stay
  • Food and snacks. There is a fridge
  • Coffee, spices etc. as needed

Power and outdoors

  • Power bank (there is no electricity on site)
  • Flashlight or headlamp with spare batteries
  • Rain gear and sturdy shoes as needed
  • Hiking map or offline map if you are visiting Nätra Fjällskog
  • Fishing gear and life jackets if you are going out in the boat
