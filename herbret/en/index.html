The cabin

A private cove by Lake Stor-Degersjön

The cabin sits in a private cove with no neighbours, in the middle of the northern forests between Örnsköldsvik and Sollefteå. It sleeps up to 4 guests, with a sofa bed downstairs and a loft with mattresses. The kitchen has a gas stove and fridge, and the cabin is heated with gas. Down by the water there is a sauna with a view and a boat for fishing.

In the middle of the northern forests, in a private cove with no neighbours.
