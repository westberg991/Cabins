The cabin

Life by Lake Bodtjärnen

Located by a private lake with no neighbours — between Örnsköldsvik and Sollefteå — the cabin gives you a rare chance to experience northern Swedish nature on your own terms. It sleeps up to 4 people with a bunk bed and a sofa bed, and is equipped with a gas-powered fridge and stove, plus a wood stove for chilly evenings.

Now and then a train passes on the far side of the lake — a quiet, atmospheric reminder that the world is out there, even if it feels impossibly far away.

Off-grid: the simple life

  • No running water. Bring your own drinking water.
  • No mains electricity or internet. Charge your power bank beforehand.
  • Outhouse and a shed with firewood on the property.

Equipment on site

  • Wood stove and grill.
  • Boat and fishing rods.
  • Picnic table, outdoor furniture and space to pitch a tent.
  • Gas-powered fridge, freezer compartment and stove.

Good to know

  • Bottom sheets are not included. Guests bring their own (see the packing list).
  • Check the coverage map in advance for mobile reception (4G/3G).
  • Bring matches for the wood stove.
Facilities

Sleep well and stay fresh

Comfortable sleeping for 4

The cabin has duvets and pillows for all 4 guests — a bunk bed or sofa bed is available depending on group size.

Making the beds: Duvets and pillows are provided. Guests bring their own bottom sheets and bedding, as many as needed for the stay.

Outdoor shower and outhouse

Next to the shed you will find a simple outdoor shower and a well-kept outhouse — with a view that is anything but ordinary.

How the shower works: Heat water indoors on the gas stove and fill a bucket. Then use the electric pump (charged via micro-USB) to shower, with the forest and the lake as your view.

Packing list

What to bring

Sleeping

  • Bottom sheets, one per person (duvets and pillows are provided)
  • Duvet covers or a sleeping bag
  • An extra blanket for chilly evenings

Hygiene and bathing

  • Towels and shower gel as needed
  • Toiletries and toilet paper
  • Insect repellent and sun protection as needed

Food and drink

  • Drinking water for the whole stay
  • Food and snacks. There is a fridge and freezer compartment
  • Coffee, spices etc. as needed

Power and outdoors

  • Power bank (there is no electricity on site)
  • Flashlight or headlamp with spare batteries
  • Rain gear and sturdy shoes as needed
  • Hiking map or offline map if you are visiting Nätra Fjällskog
